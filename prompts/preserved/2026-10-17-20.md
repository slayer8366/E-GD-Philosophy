## To confirm with the owner

Practised by the planners, and written in their handoffs and profiles, but not found in the owner's own words in Forager's record, preserved prompts, docs or commit messages (searched at records-after-173 0335c015 and pre-main b774cac5). Each goes in only once the owner confirms it, in their own words, in the planner's window:

1. Explain in behaviour and logic, not code names (the owner understands systems, and does not read code).
2. Give times in Pacific; the record stays in UTC.
3. Ask for clarity whenever unsure; no apology needed.
4. Record a decision once; never record a re-ask that changed nothing, a misreading, or the explaining that fixed it.
5. The owner's screenshots stay off GitHub.
6. Other products, apps and device makers are described generically.
7. One plain question per decision, the recommendation first and marked "(Recommended)", up to four at a time.
8. Lead with the outcome and keep it short; a table when comparing; honest timings and bad news; slips admitted plainly; end with the one decision needed.
9. Read an answer literally: "Option 1, but ..." is a new ruling, and a question back gets an explanation.
10. An override is the owner's, once; it changes no rule, and no agent treats it as a precedent.
11. "Undo" covers the step just taken, not earlier ones the owner asked for.
12. Saying what will happen to something ("it will be deleted") is policy, not an instruction to do it.
