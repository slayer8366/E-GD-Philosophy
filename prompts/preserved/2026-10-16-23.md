# Proposal: Forager's production gate and auditor, in E-GD (nothing written yet)

- **Branches** (Forager -1086, -1087):

  - experiments live on production plus developer tools, rebased after each release and never merged.
- **The gate** has three steps:
  1. automated checks pass at the exact commit being promoted;
  2. the auditor's report;
  3. your approval for public use.
  - A device check or field walk is proposed when a promotion needs one, and you decide; it isn't a standing step (-1133).
  - An urgent fix takes the same gate, at the front of the queue.
- **The auditor:**

  - it walks every changed behaviour as a step path;

  - "Advises, you decide": it writes no code, fixes or merges nothing, never weakens a test, and never settles a conflict between your rulings.
