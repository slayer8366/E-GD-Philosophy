## Candidate lessons

Grouped by the home proposed for them, each with a generic wording. "n" is the instance count and how it was counted. Evidence quotes are verbatim; record IDs refer to Forager unless marked forecast. The A and B tags say which reader found each one.

### Promotions: PROTOCOL rules the evidence now supports as principles

**P1. Verify the premises before building** (PROTOCOL rule 3).
- *Generic:* Before building, the executor checks each premise of its task against the source, citing file and line, and reports which are false. This catches errors in the plan that the planner's own access to the source did not prevent.
- *Evidence:*
  - -1457: "caught by Hazel".
  - -1986: the planner's option "rested on two wrong premises".
  - -2026: "verify-first corrected the planner".
  - forager-forecast and Forager reports: 4 of 8 new verify-first reports carry a wrong or incomplete premise [pattern, B].
- *n:* 112 entries, 35 wrong premises, 21 of them traced to the planner or the dispatch [pattern, A].
- *Home:* promote rule 3, or amend Early Flagging of Ambiguities.

**P2. An independent review, on a different model** (PROTOCOL rule 11).
- *Generic:* A result later work stands on, and each change before it is promoted, is reviewed by a session that did not build it, running on a different model.
- *Evidence:*
  - -1134: "It's useful because the same model shares blind spots."
  - Two forecast reviews on two models independently found the same two medium findings (`docs/audits/2026-10-21-t8-fit/review-fable.md`, `review-sonnet.md`) [B].
  - Release audits caught the planner's own gaps 4 times in 13 [A].
  - -1617: "the figure reads short".
  - Related work S27 and S28 (`paper/related-work.md`) give independent support.
- *n:* 201 entries; 13 blocking or should-fix items [pattern]; 2 converging review pairs [read].
- *Home:* promote rule 11 with the different-model clause.
- *Also in scope:* forager-forecast's reviews were filed as "a faithful condensation of the reviewer's final message". A reviewer that may not write still has its words kept verbatim [B, n=2].

### Amendments to rules already written

**M1. Rule 12, what a gate costs: whole kinds lapse, not only fields.**
- *Generic:*
  - Without a checker, required entry kinds stop being written across a change of session, not only required fields.
  - A rule written down after a slip does not hold unless something checks it where it applies.
  - A rule that names its own enforcement says where that enforcement runs.
  - A one-time override records what it stopped enforcing.
- *Evidence:*
  - **Kinds lapsed:** intents, terminals and outcomes, 10-24 to 11-02; merges after -1609; corrections after 10-20 [script, A].
  - **Breaches of written rules:** -1998 set a rule for phone sessions, and -2064 reports "The planner's slip, a third time today". 8 breaches of 4 rules in all [read, A].
  - **A rule naming enforcement that didn't run:** the modular-code skill says "CI warns on a PR that grows a file past these limits", and no such CI step exists at `6e24b22c4` [pattern, B].
  - **An override's gap:** -1354, "This is a one-case override, not a rule change", was followed by an unreported red run [B].
- *Home:* rule 12, as evidence and wording.

**M2. Rule 13, parallel work collides on shared state.**
- *Generic:*
  - Identifiers shared between records are partitioned by namespace, not by numeric range. A range is a forecast of the other record's growth.
  - An ID that has been removed stays retired.
  - Where several planners share one branch, every merge is recorded where the others read before they act.
- *Evidence:*
  - **Range partition:** the 43-ID collision above [script].
  - **Removed IDs:** -1963 to -1967 reused, and -1342 dangling [git].
  - **Shared branch:** "None had an entry here first" (Forager RECORD.md at `477dbc7a`, cited by B).
  - **Handovers:** -1422, "Two planner sessions ran in parallel for about 20 minutes", and 5 handover collisions in all [read, A].
- *Home:* rule 13.

**M3. Rule 23, the release gate.**
- *Generic:*
  - Every check is bound to the artifact that lands: the commit, tree or hash checked is shown equal to what shipped.
  - A device check run on a build other than the one shipped is listed as a gap.
  - A register of known defects ships with each release, with a rule for which may ship.
  - A gate's verdicts are counted; one that has only ever passed has not been shown able to fail.
- *Evidence:*
  - -1421: "so the checked tree is the landed tree".
  - -2135: "Every S22 build was tools/s22-combined".
  - `docs/process/known-bugs.md`: 23 rows.
  - 11 of 11 audits read "pass with items" [script, B].
  - The standing note "No release with code has yet been audited" is out of date.
- *n:* 64 merges bound to the checked head or tree; 84 hash or tree checks [pattern, A].
- *Home:* rule 23 wording and standing.

**M4. Operator Authority: the recommended option, and restatements.**
- *Generic:*
  - When the operator answers through options, the recommended one usually decides.
  - A recommendation is checked against the operator's standing words before it is offered. The operator's own words are the first option, and no option that departs from a standing ruling is marked recommended.
  - A planner's restatement of a ruling is labelled as the planner's and never carried forward as the ruling.
  - Changing settings or data on the operator's device is a decision, whether or not it can be undone.
- *Evidence:*
  - **The recommendation decides:** 814 recommended answers against 4 recorded departures [pattern, A]. -2068: the planner offered a recommended option "instead of going back to the owner's words".
  - **Restatement becomes the ruling:** -2073, "The red was never the owner's choice"; -2034, a condition "was the planner's own, not the owner's". 7 cases in all [read, A].
  - **The operator's device:** -2072, "Whether a change can be undone doesn't make it permitted", and 4 rulings in all [read, A].
  - **Forager's own rule:** "The owner's word" in CLAUDE.md at `6e24b22c4` [B].
- *Home:* Operator Authority amendment.

### New PROTOCOL candidates

- **N1. Exhausted resources break checks silently, and an exit code is not a state.**
  - *Generic:* Disk, memory, temporary storage and usage limits are shared state every check depends on. Free space is checked before a gated step. A tool's output is checked for completeness. Success is read from the resulting state, not the exit code. Gated steps are chained so that a failure stops the next one.
  - *Evidence:*
    - -1516: a tool "stops at about 110.7 MB and still exits 0".
    - -1417: a backup failed for lack of space and the merge still ran; then "From here every gated step is joined so a failure stops the next".
    - -1905: "The laptop-only evidence those folders held is gone".
  - *n:* 18 exhaustion events; 2 failed open; 3 deleted evidence to recover space [read, A]. Plus 6 machine limits that became rules [B].
- **N2. A refused or interrupted action may have partly run.**
  - *Generic:* After a refusal, interruption or failure, the actual state is read before anything is run again.
  - *Evidence:* -1930: "The tool call was shown as rejected"; the command was re-run and both landed. -2076.
  - *n:* 3 [read, A].
- **N3. Process documents drift against each other.**
  - *Generic:* A clause saying which document wins settles a conflict once someone finds it. It does not find it. A session outside the project cross-reads the process documents at intervals.
  - *Evidence:* five contradictions across the two projects' process documents. Two of them were resolved by the operator on 2026-11-04 (`9b58c66a4`, `df3ab14ab`), and three still stood at the commits read.
  - *n:* 5 [read, B].
- **N4. A ruling about process lives in the governing document.**
  - *Generic:* An operator ruling that changes how work is done is written, with its words, into the document that governs the work before it is applied. A copy in an agent's notes is a pointer, not the rule.
  - *Evidence:*
    - Forager's in-place correction rule has no quoted ruling in the record range [pattern, A]. It is in the planner profile.
    - A forecast document: "The memory rule … was also missed at first" [B].
  - *n:* 6 [read, A] plus 1 [B].
- **N5. Hand off at a context threshold, carrying the record.**
  - *Generic:* A session hands off past a set context size. The handoff names the dispatch, the record entries and the branch heads, not a summary. All work is pushed first.
  - *Evidence:*
    - -1645: "a fresh helper past about 200,000 tokens".
    - -2161: a helper "went well past the ~200k handover point (about 677k tokens)".
    - -1619 on usage limits.
  - *n:* 12 applications and 1 exception [read, A]; 4 handoffs [B]; 7 usage-limit interruptions [read, A].
- **N6. Points of no return are named in the plan and asked at the point.**
  - *Evidence:* forecast planner profile, "never assumed from an earlier go".
  - *n:* 2 [B].
- **N7. Code that refuses rather than choosing.**
  - *Generic:* When the data needs a reading the fixed rules don't give, the program stops and names what needs a ruling.
  - *Evidence:* forecast decisions D135 and D137 came from such refusals.
  - *n:* 2 [B].
