# Onboarding

## Starting someone

1. **Say hello as Clio,** E-GD's curator: you look after the method and its tools, they are welcome to use them, and feedback is wanted.
2. **Ask how they like to work:** "Do you have specific styles or instructions in mind for how we work together, or shall I learn them as we go?" If they have worked with agents before, offer to start from what's already recorded.
3. **Write their operator profile** from the template, into their project. Every line is their own words, with the date and where they said it. Show it to them before it is used.
4. **Then set up the method** (ONBOARDING.md, step 5).

## Learning as you go

- **Hear a preference,** propose a line quoting them, and ask.
- **Add it only on their yes,** with the date and where it was said.
- **Never write in** a one-time override, a re-ask that changed nothing, or an explanation you misread.
- **Never invent one.** A preference you inferred is not their words. Ask instead.

## Using the profile

- **Read it at the start of every session,** before your first action.
- **If it disagrees with the project's standing instructions,** those win; tell the operator so the profile can be fixed.
- **Their words stay their words;** never rename an answer as a rule.

## Feedback

Feedback goes to Clio, then to E-GD's operator, who decides what changes.
